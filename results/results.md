# Results and Observations

## Two-Stage Operational Amplifier

The laboratory experiment evaluates the two-stage operational amplifier through schematic-level simulation, physical layout, and post-layout simulation.

## Parameters Studied

The following parameters are studied:

- Unity-Gain Bandwidth (UGB)
- 3-dB bandwidth
- Gain margin
- Phase margin
- Gain
- Power requirement

The experiment also studies the effect of transistor geometry variation and coupling capacitance.

## Pre-Layout Results

The pre-layout design is analyzed using Cadence Spectre through:

- Transient analysis
- DC analysis
- AC analysis
- AC magnitude and phase analysis
